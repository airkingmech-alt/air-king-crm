## Original behavior (superseded by the approximate-efficiency policy below)

Complete AC/furnace/coil selections are evaluated as four fixed option identities:

| Saved identity | Offering | Coverage | Added labor warranty job cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Value | Cheapest reviewed compatible Guardian AC + Guardian furnace + selected Champion coil | 10-year parts only | $0 |
| Good | Champion XC3, 13.4 SEER2 series + selected Champion furnace and coil | 10-year parts and labor | $250 |
| Better | Champion XC4, 14.3 SEER2 series + same Champion furnace and coil | 10-year parts and labor | $250 |
| Best | Champion XC6, 16+ SEER2 series + same single-stage furnace for 80%, or two-stage Champion furnace retaining selected AFUE for high efficiency + same coil | 10-year parts and labor | $250 |

Candidate substitutions preserve selected cooling capacity, furnace input/cabinet/blower code, AFUE, coil, accessories and labor/material/scope inputs. Furnace catalog tonnage is not used as cooling airflow. Guardian selection sorts actual compatible model costs, without multipliers. A reviewed exact condenser/coil/furnace match is required. The top option also requires exact-model furnace AFUE and two-stage evidence in the match registry. Series efficiency labels are separate from the reviewed matched-system SEER2 rating.

The builder shows all four candidate cards with exact blockers. Only valid options are saved. If none is valid, saving is blocked. Blockers for omitted options are retained in the draft's internal review note. No blocked candidate is presented as a purchasable customer option. Existing saved quotes are never regenerated by rendering. Heat pump, mini-split, maintenance, and non-complete-AC-system paths retain their existing behavior.

The public/staff layouts support four cards (one column on mobile, two on medium screens, four on large screens). Four-card print/PDF layouts use two columns for legibility. The fourth identity passes through existing option-based acceptance, invoice, and scheduling paths. A local migration raises the database edit guard from three to four options and permits Value; it changes no customer rows. It aborts if the expected current guard has drifted. Deploying this code requires that migration to be reviewed and applied under separate approval.
